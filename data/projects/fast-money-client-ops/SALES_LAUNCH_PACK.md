# The WhatIf Company - First-Customer Sales Launch Pack
Status: draft for owner review; no live offer, payments, or outreach authorized.

## First sellable offer: Client Follow-Up & Scheduling Starter
Target: small cleaning, property management, and field-service businesses with recurring inquiries.
Proposed price: $1,500 fixed-scope implementation; $750 after signed scope/approval, $750 after acceptance. Optional support $200/month under separate agreement.
Delivery estimate: 7-14 business days after access, requirements, and deposit; confirm capacity before promising.

### Included (scope to validate with each buyer)
1. Intake form or existing lead-source import for one approved source.
2. Simple operator dashboard for leads, contact status, and next action.
3. One appointment-request workflow (not a guarantee of automatic booking).
4. Two approved follow-up templates and a manual-approval or opt-in sending flow.
5. Basic handoff/training session and written operating instructions.
6. Acceptance walkthrough covering one test lead, follow-up, and job handoff.

### Excluded unless separately quoted
SMS/WhatsApp fees, phone agents, custom mobile apps, third-party subscriptions, unlimited integrations, 24/7 support, payment processing, and promised sales lift. Any automatic external messages require customer authorization and applicable consent.

## Qualification questions
- How many customer inquiries arrive each week, and through which channels?
- What happens after an inquiry arrives? Where do inquiries get lost?
- Who books appointments and follows up, and how much time does it take?
- Which system holds customer data today? Who can authorize access?
- What would a successful 30-day pilot look like?
- Is there a budget and decision-maker for a $1,500 fixed-scope pilot?

## Discovery-call script (5-10 minutes)
'I help service businesses organize customer inquiries and repetitive follow-ups. I am not proposing to replace your staff. Could you walk me through what happens between a new inquiry and a confirmed appointment? If there is a measurable bottleneck, I can scope a small fixed-price pilot and show a relevant demo.'

## Proposal outline
Customer / date / owner contact
Current workflow and observed problem (customer-confirmed facts only)
Proposed solution and exactly one lead source
Deliverables and acceptance criteria
Timeline and dependencies
Fixed price and milestone payments
Customer data/security, approved messaging channels and third-party fees
Support boundaries and change-request process
Signatures / approval

## Internal demo acceptance checklist
[ ] Seeded fictional prospect only; clearly labeled demo data.
[ ] Lead enters workflow and is visible in operator view.
[ ] Operator updates lead status and assigns next action.
[ ] Quote approval and job creation verified.
[ ] Invoice lifecycle tested with sandbox credentials, not real charges.
[ ] Follow-up preview does not send externally.
[ ] Mobile and desktop manual walkthrough recorded.
[ ] Production authentication and data protection reviewed before hosting.

## Outreach gate
Research 30-50 legitimate businesses, deduplicate and verify public contact channels, draft individual messages based on observed facts, and require owner approval before each live send. Record opt-outs and suppression. No bulk unsolicited sends.

## First month measurement
Prospects researched; relevant conversations; discovery calls; approved proposals; signed agreements; collected payments; delivery hours/costs; recurring revenue. Do not report pipeline or test transactions as revenue.
