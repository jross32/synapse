# Superiority Claim Gate`n`nNever infer overall superiority from subsystem tests. claim_gate.py requires at least five paired repeats with the same model/version, tools, machine, fixture, task, prompt, and time budget. Every pair needs complete evidence, zero challenger critical failures, challenger pass rate no lower than baseline, and a strict challenger score win. Ties, inconsistent wins, incomplete evidence, or environment mismatches refuse the claim. Passing supports only the measured benchmark configuration, not universal superiority.
